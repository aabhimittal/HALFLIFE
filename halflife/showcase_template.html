Run it yourself

Everything above came from the offline simulated host and regenerates with one command. To measure a real assistant, swap in its consolidator.

What these numbers are. They come from HALFLIFE's simulated host: a parameterized model of merge, paraphrase, compression and forgetting. They show what the instrument measures and how defenses interact. They are not measurements of any vendor's product. Each assumption is a setting; see docs/METHODOLOGY.md.
